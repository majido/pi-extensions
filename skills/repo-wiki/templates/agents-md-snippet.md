## Repo Wiki

Project documentation lives in [docs/wiki/index.md](docs/wiki/index.md).

- Read the index at session start; open **only** the pages relevant to your task.
- If your change invalidates a wiki page, update the affected sections (surgical edits,
  not rewrites) in the same PR and refresh the page's `last_verified` / `verified_against`
  frontmatter.
- New wiki pages must be linked from the index in the same commit.
- Skip updates for cosmetic, test-only, or purely internal refactoring changes.
